## 01 — Cavalo em tinta

Use case: logo-brand. Create ONE original full-body galloping horse for Hunter Lab, proposal 1 'Cavalo em tinta'. Reference images provided in this conversation are STYLE references only: black horse on white with red stars; cream horse on black; energetic black brush horse on red. Do not copy their silhouettes, poses, stars or graphic compositions. Design a NEW expressive equine silhouette, entire horse clearly visible including four legs and hooves, strong chest, anatomically convincing horse head with muzzle and ears, flying mane and long flowing tail. Horse galloping LEFT with front legs gathering and rear legs extending, a dynamic suspended stride. Organic confident hand-ink curves, subtly broken brush edges on mane/tail only, strong connected black masses, very sparse internal white cuts. Not polygonal or geometric, not a head logo, not a rearing horse, no letters, no text, no stars, no badge, no lightning, no gear. Flat BLACK INK on pure WHITE. Center the complete horse with 12% whitespace all sides; horizontal silhouette. Premium expressive silkscreen artwork that can be manually vectorized, clean minimal background, no shadows, no grayscale, no gradients. Preserve broad leg separations for Android 48px legibility; no tiny splatter. This is a new artistic identity, not a corporate automotive logo.
